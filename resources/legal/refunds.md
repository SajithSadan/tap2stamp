This policy explains free trials, cancellations and refunds for Tada Tap subscriptions and for the physical products we sell, such as counter display stands and QR stickers. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## Free trial

- Every new shop gets a **free trial of {trial_days} days**.
- You don't need to give any payment details to start the trial, and nothing is charged during it.
- When the trial ends, you can choose a paid plan to keep using Tada Tap. If you don't, your account can be paused, but nothing is charged.

## Subscriptions

### Refunds within {refund_days} days

If you buy a subscription and change your mind, ask for a refund **within {refund_days} days of the payment date**. You'll get a **full refund** of that payment, and the subscription ends.

### Cancelling after {refund_days} days

After {refund_days} days you can still **cancel your subscription at any time**, including a yearly plan.

- Cancelling stops any further renewals.
- You keep using Tada Tap until the end of the period you've already paid for.
- The amount paid for the current period is **not refunded**, in full or in part.

### Renewals

Subscriptions run for the period shown when you buy them (for example, one year). We'll tell you before a renewal is charged. To stop a renewal, cancel before the renewal date.

## Physical products

These include counter display stands, NFC/QR stands and QR stickers.

### Refunds within {refund_days} days

- Ask for a refund **within {refund_days} days of receiving your order**.
- Products should be unused and in their original condition and packaging.
- We'll tell you how to send them back. Once we receive them, we refund the price you paid.

### Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, tell us within {refund_days} days of delivery and send a photo if you can. We'll replace it or refund it in full, including any delivery costs, at no extra cost to you.

### After {refund_days} days

After {refund_days} days we can't offer a refund for change of mind. If a product develops a fault through normal use, contact us and we'll help.

## How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the **payment method you used**. For bank transfers, we pay into the account you tell us.
- We process them within 5 working days of approving the refund (or of receiving a returned product).
- Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
- Coupons and discounts aren't refunded as cash; you get back what you actually paid.
