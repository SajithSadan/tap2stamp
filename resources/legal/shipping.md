This policy covers how we deliver physical products bought on Tada Tap, such as counter display stands, NFC/QR stands and QR stickers. The Tada Tap app itself is delivered online straight away; there's nothing to ship.

## Where we deliver

- **Physical products are sent to addresses in the United Kingdom only.**
- Outside the UK, you can download your shop's QR codes from your dashboard and print them yourself.

## Delivery address

- We send your order to the delivery address on your account, or your shop address if you haven't given a separate one.
- Check it before you order: your dashboard shows it on the order page.
- If you need to change it after ordering, contact us before the order is dispatched.

## When it arrives

- We aim to dispatch orders within **3 working days** of payment.
- UK delivery usually takes a further **2 to 5 working days**.
- Busy periods, public holidays and courier delays can make it longer.
- Your dashboard shows each order's progress: received, processing, dispatched and delivered.
- When an order is dispatched, we email you the courier and tracking details where available.

## Delivery costs

Any delivery charge is shown before you pay.

## Problems with a delivery

- **Not arrived:** if your order hasn't arrived within 10 working days of dispatch, contact us and we'll trace it, or send a replacement.
- **Damaged or wrong item:** tell us within {refund_days} days of delivery. See our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds).
