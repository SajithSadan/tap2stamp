This policy explains what personal data Tada Tap (tadatap.app) collects, why, who we share it with, and your rights.

**Who provides Tada Tap:** {company}.{company_details}

## Our role

- **Shop owners and staff:** we decide how your account data is used, so we are the **controller** for it.
- **Customers of a shop:**
  - The **shop** is the controller of your data: you join that shop's loyalty card, and the shop decides how to use your details.
  - We are the shop's **processor**: we store and process your data for the shop, under its instructions, to run the loyalty card.
  - You can contact the shop or us about your data.

## What we collect

### From shop owners
- **Account:** name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash) or Google sign-in ID.
- **Business details:** business name, type, contact person, phone number, shop and delivery addresses, logo, banner, menu and settings.
- **Orders and payments:** what you bought, amounts and delivery details.
  - Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
- **Support:** messages between us and you, and our notes from those conversations.

### From staff
- Name.
- A PIN, stored only as a secure hash.
- Which stamps and rewards they gave, and when.

### From customers of a shop
- **Name and mobile number,** given when you join a shop's loyalty card.
- **Loyalty activity:** stamps, rewards, and when you visited.
- **Ratings and feedback** you leave for a shop.
- **WhatsApp offers:** whether you agreed to receive them from that shop (and when), and your unsubscribe if you later opt out.
- **A random card ID** saved in your browser, so you don't have to sign up again. This is not a password.

### Automatically
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you use.
  - We use these to keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse.
  - We also use the country your IP address is in to show prices in your currency.
- **Cookies and browser storage:** see [Cookies](#cookies) below.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Running Tada Tap: accounts, loyalty cards, stamps, rewards, menus, staff access | Contract (shop owners); the shop's legitimate interests (customers) |
| Taking payments and delivering products | Contract; legal obligations (tax and accounting records) |
| Sending a shop's WhatsApp offers | Your consent, given on that shop's card; you can withdraw it at any time |
| Support, onboarding help and service messages to shop owners | Contract; our legitimate interests |
| Security, fraud prevention, fixing problems, improving the service | Our legitimate interests |
| Insights for shop owners (visits, busy times, returning customers) | The shop's legitimate interests |

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use customers' data for our own advertising.

## Artificial intelligence

Some features use Google's Gemini AI:
- **Reading a menu photo:** the photo you upload is sent to Gemini.
- **Matching menu items to a voice or text command:** the menu item names and the command are sent.
- **Checking menu photos:** the candidate photos are sent.
- **Helping our team prepare support calls:** shop-level figures and our notes are sent.

**Customers' names and phone numbers are never sent to the AI.**

## Who we share data with

We use trusted providers to run Tada Tap. They process data only for us, or for the shop:

| Provider | What for |
|---|---|
| Hostinger | Hosting, database and email sending |
| Stripe | Card payments |
| Pusher | Live updates of stamps on a customer's card |
| Google | Sign in with Google, Gemini AI features, and web fonts |
| Meta (WhatsApp) | Sending a shop's WhatsApp offers to customers who opted in |
| ipinfo.io | Working out a visitor's country from their IP address, to show the right currency |
| findaddress.io | Looking up UK addresses from a postcode when a shop owner fills in their address |
| YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) | Our demo video, which only loads if you press play |

- **When we must:** we may also share data if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users' safety, or with a buyer if our business is sold. In that case this policy keeps applying.
- **International transfers:** some of these providers process data outside your country, for example in the United States or the European Union. Where needed, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Shop owner and staff accounts | While the account is active, then up to 12 months after it closes |
| Customer loyalty data | While the shop uses Tada Tap. A shop can ask us to delete its customers' data at any time; you can ask the shop or us |
| WhatsApp opt-outs | Kept, so you aren't messaged again |
| Orders and payment records | As long as tax and accounting law requires (usually 6 years) |
| Security and activity logs | Up to 12 months |

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under UK GDPR, EU GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you can:
- ask for a copy of your data;
- ask us to correct or delete it;
- object to or restrict some uses;
- ask to move it;
- withdraw consent at any time, for example by using the unsubscribe link in any WhatsApp offer.

**How to ask:**
- Email {email}.
- If you're a customer of a shop, you can also ask the shop.

We may need to check who you are first, and we reply within one month.

**Complaints:** you can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)). We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

## Cookies

Tada Tap uses only **essential** cookies and browser storage, the ones the service needs to work:
- **Sign-in session and security cookies:** a session cookie and an anti-forgery (CSRF) cookie.
- **"Keep me signed in" cookie:** only if you choose to stay signed in.
- **Browser storage:**
  - your loyalty card ID;
  - the last shop you visited;
  - a staff device's sign-in;
  - small preferences, such as a dismissed prompt or table layout.

We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. YouTube's privacy-enhanced player loads only if you press play on our demo video.

## Children

Tada Tap is not meant for children under 13. Shops shouldn't sign up customers under 13 without a parent's consent.

## Security

- We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and hashed passwords and PINs.
- Every shop's data is kept separate from every other shop's.
- Access is limited to people who need it.

No service can be completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will tell you and the authorities about a serious breach where the law requires.
